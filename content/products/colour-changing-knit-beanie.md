## The piece

A ribbed beanie knitted from the same thermochromic yarn as our long sleeve and hoodie.
Cold air deepens the colour; a warm room brings it back.

## Why we made it

It is the most visible way to show what heat-reactive yarn does - your hands and head are
the first things to feel a change in temperature, so the effect reads instantly.
