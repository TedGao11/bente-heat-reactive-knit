<summary>Description</summary>

<p>A heavier knit built from colourworked panels, so the pattern runs through the garment instead of sitting on the surface. It is knitted in our own mill rather than bought in and printed, which is why the colours hold their shape wash after wash.</p>

<ul>
<li>Heavyweight hand-feel with a soft brushed interior</li>
<li>Ribbed collar, cuffs and hem</li>
<li>Drop shoulder, relaxed body</li>
<li>Colourwork is knitted into the fabric, so it cannot peel or crack</li>
</ul>
