## The piece

Our hoodie takes the same heat-reactive yarn and puts it into a heavier knit. Step out
into the cold and the colour deepens; come back inside and it turns again.

## Details

- Brushed interior for warmth without bulk
- Ribbed cuffs and hem
- Kangaroo pocket
- Colour change is knitted in, not printed on

## Why it matters

Most colour-changing hoodies on the market are made by coating or printing a finished
garment, which is why the effect fades. Ours is knitted from thermochromic yarn, so the
colour change is part of the fabric itself.
